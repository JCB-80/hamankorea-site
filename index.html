About · 회사 소개

One engineering team, three connected lines of work

HamanKorea (하만코리아) is an independent business registered in the Republic of Korea, engaged in electrical and electronic engineering research and development, product design, and device manufacturing. Our work is carried out end to end — hardware, firmware, mobile applications, and the content systems around them — from our base in Asan, Chungcheongnam-do.

Solutions · 주요 사업

What we build

Three lines of work share one engineering team: a diagnostic product line, an automation pipeline for multilingual media, and the hardware research that supports both.

Mobile AI · Diagnostics

CODEPOP

코드팝 — Wi-Fi 현미경 카메라 기반 모바일 피부·두피 진단 AI 솔루션

CODEPOP connects a Wi-Fi microscope camera to a mobile app to capture close-up skin and scalp imagery and support guided examination on a phone.

  • Wireless (Wi-Fi) microscope camera paired with the mobile app
  • Skin and scalp examination for everyday care
  • Officially launched in January 2024
  • Continuously developed on both iOS and Android
AI · Media automation

AI Content & Media Automation

다국어 뉴스 큐레이션 및 AI 숏폼·비디오 자동화 파이프라인

A production pipeline that curates multilingual IT, automotive, and lifestyle news and turns it into short-form and video content on a daily schedule.

  • Multilingual news curation and summarisation (IT, automotive, lifestyle)

  • Scheduled daily output across multiple formats
  • Applied in-house to our own publishing channels
